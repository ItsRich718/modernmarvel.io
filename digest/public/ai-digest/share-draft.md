**Subject:** Modern Marvel, edition 001: seven newsletters reported and two counted

Hello,

I have started publishing something I have been building privately: a weekly
read of the AI newsletter cycle that tries to separate genuine agreement from
coverage volume.

The premise is simple. Three aggregators reporting the same launch is one press
release counted three times, so the system only credits a theme when sources
that do their own thinking arrive at it separately, and it strips sponsored
placements out before counting independence at all.

Edition 001 is here: https://modernmarvel.io/ai-digest

The finding this week is less about AI than about the method. Seven newsletters
reported. Six of ten tracked themes converged. But only two publishers supplied
the independent evidence that let any of them qualify, and every finding on the
page rests on two specific issues, the earliest of which expires two days after
publication.

One title published exactly on schedule and contributed nothing to the count,
because the only item it carried on a tracked theme was a paid placement. That
is the sponsored rule doing its job, and it is the part I would most like a
second opinion on. The same week, the theme with the heaviest advertising turned
out to have the least independent support behind it.

It is also a design exercise. Each edition applies a different layout directive
to the same data, and the archive is meant to be read as a series of design
arguments. This first one is an exception and says so on the page: the rotation
is inside its own cooldown until September 7.
